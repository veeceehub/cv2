What I Bring to the Table

Twenty years.
Sharpened into four things.

Agency side, brand side, consulting side. Beauty, FMCG, direct-selling, luxury watches, healthcare. What travels across all of it isn't a playbook. It's a way of working.

  • Marketing that sits at the P&L table. I've been in the rooms where business strategy takes shape, not the ones where comms gets briefed afterwards. I speak revenue, margin and mix before I speak campaign.
  • A builder's instinct. Built an agency from zero to 45 people. Took Timex DTC to 8X and 35% organic brand growth. Took a website 7.5X in two quarters. I'm at my best where there's something to build.
  • A category-agnostic brand lens. Five categories, three vantage points, one consistent discipline: find the truth about the consumer, then make the business act on it.
  • People-first leadership, without the poster. Teams do their best work when they're trusted and protected. I've built those teams, and I've walked away from environments that made them impossible.
